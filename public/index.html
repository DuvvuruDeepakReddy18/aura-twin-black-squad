WELCOME TO AURA TWIN

See the whole picture.
Then look ahead.

An interactive, synthetic patient digital twin for exploring two-hour glucose forecasts.

  1. 01

    Choose a patient

    Each fictional patient combines a historical health record with a five-minute wearable stream.

  2. 02

    Move through their day

    Play or scrub the timeline. The model only sees observations at or before the selected moment.

  3. 03

    Understand the forecast

    Inspect the future curve, uncertainty interval, contributing inputs and model evidence. Scenario changes are illustrative.

  4. 04

    Document the observation

    Acknowledge an insight, save a note locally and export a summary for your prototype walkthrough.
