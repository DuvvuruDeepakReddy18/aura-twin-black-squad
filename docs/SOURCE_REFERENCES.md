# Source references and attribution

Application code uses the MIT License. The following data and publications retain their own terms and attribution requirements.

1. Cho, P., Kim, J., Bent, B., and Dunn, J. (2026). **BIG IDEAs Lab Glycemic Variability and Wearable Device Data**, version 1.1.3. PhysioNet. [Dataset and license](https://physionet.org/content/big-ideas-glycemic-wearable/1.1.3/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.13026/aw6y-fc44). The imported subset is CGM, HR, IBI and matched study demographics; other dataset channels are not silently represented as imported.
2. Bent, B., Cho, P. J., Henriquez, M., et al. (2021). **Engineering digital biomarkers of interstitial glucose from noninvasive smartwatches.** npj Digital Medicine, 4, 89. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-021-00465-w). This is the source study, not a validation of AURA's model.
3. Pollard, T., et al. (2026). **PhysioNet as a global platform for biomedical research.** Nature Health. [Platform citation](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44360-026-00096-z).
4. Walonoski, J., et al. (2018). **Synthea: An approach, method, and software mechanism for generating synthetic patients and the synthetic electronic health care record.** Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association. [Publication](https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocx079) · [Official sample data](https://synthetichealth.github.io/downloads.html) · [CSV dictionary](https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea/wiki/CSV-File-Data-Dictionary).

Synthea profiles and wearable participants are different people. Their composite assignment does not establish identity linkage or clinical fusion. The [source manifest](../data/research-provenance.json) and [Synthea provenance](SYNTHEA_PROVENANCE.md) record the pinned files, transformations and source-data terms.
